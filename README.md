# UyirThulir
## AI-Enabled Livestock Health Early-Warning & Surveillance Platform

UyirThulir is a full-stack livestock health surveillance platform designed to connect field-level animal health reporting with AI-assisted risk assessment, veterinary investigation, laboratory validation, government surveillance, GIS intelligence and notifications.

The platform is designed for early warning and coordinated surveillance. It does **not** perform autonomous disease diagnosis.

---

# 1. Project Overview

Livestock diseases can spread across farms and locations before formal confirmation is available.

UyirThulir provides a digital workflow for collecting early health observations and moving them through the appropriate professional response chain.
